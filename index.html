      Korea cheat sheet

      • 💵 Cash for markets (Gwangjang, Noryangjin, Tongin, Myeongdong)
      • 🚇 Buy a T-money card at any convenience store for the subway
      • 🗺️ Naver Map & KakaoMap > Google Maps for directions inside Korea
      • 📱 The "Copy Korean" button on each stop copies the Korean name for taxi drivers
      • 🍶 Tipping is not customary anywhere

      How to edit

      • Drag a card in a day group to reorder, or drop it into another day's section to move it.
      • Tap a card to see details, then tap ✏️ Edit to change anything.
      • + Add place searches OpenStreetMap. 📍 Drop pin lets you tap the map.
      • Drag a pin on the map to reposition.
      • 🔗 Book on each card opens the reservation site directly.
      • Tap the booking pill to cycle none → needs booking → ✅ booked.
      • Export / Import / Reset are in the toolbar above the cards.

      Data lives in localStorage on this device. Tap ⬇ Export before switching phones/laptops, then ⬆ Import on the other device.
